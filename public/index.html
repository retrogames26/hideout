HOW TO PLAY

Everyone must allow location access. Your GPS position is shared with players in this room while the round is active.

Hiders: stay inside the hiding zone. If you leave its circle, seekers get a GPS ping to your approximate location.

Tagging: when a seeker requests a catch, the hider confirms with “I was caught”.

The map uses OpenStreetMap street data.

Play outdoors only, obey local rules, and never cross roads or enter private property to chase someone.
